Glossary

Form 4

The official SEC filing for a change in beneficial ownership by a Section 16 insider. Instructions: sec.gov/files/form4.pdf.

Code P / S / A / M / F

Official Form 4 Item 8 codes: P = open-market or private purchase; S = open-market or private sale; A = grant/award under Rule 16b-3(d); M = derivative exercise/conversion; F = share withholding for tax or exercise price.

ROI / Sharpe / win rate

Simulation statistics from the research backtester. They are not live brokerage results and are blank until official Form 4 rows have been collected.
